## Key discovery: `reading_id` encodes the slot

`reading_id = R` + (hour_index × 56 + (flat − 1) × 7 + channel), 1-based, where hour_index counts hours from 2026-10-01T00:00Z. The formula reproduces the reported flat, appliance and timestamp for 37,679 of 37,715 rows and the timestamp for **all** rows (0 timestamp disagreements). It is therefore used as independent evidence to repair identifiers — only when at least two of the three fields (reading_id, flat_id, appliance_id) agree with each other, the timestamp agrees, and the target slot has no other record.

### Conflicting duplicates

In 24 of 25 conflicts the two copies differ by exactly **+0.13 kWh** — a systematic offset, not measurement noise. The copy closer to the median of the same channel's ±2 neighbouring hours was kept; this equals the lower copy in 25 of 25 cases. Maximum effect if the other copy were right: 3.25 kWh in total (0.17 % of observed consumption).

### Spike threshold

The largest plausible reading is 1.30 kWh/h; the smallest rejected value is 10.8 kWh/h, so any threshold between ~1.5 and 10 kWh gives the same result. Rejected spikes are not Wh mislabelled as kWh: read as Wh they would be 0.01–0.045 kWh, which is impossible for e.g. the always-on cold appliance (≥0.035 kWh/h) and heater channels in flats that never use a heater.

## Daylight-saving transition (25 October)

- Local offsets present: {'+02:00': 4616, '+01:00': 760}.
- `timestamp_local` agrees with the IANA `Europe/Stockholm` conversion of `timestamp_utc` on every row (0 mismatches) and denotes the same instant (0 mismatches).
- The repeated wall-clock hour is legitimate: [['2026-10-25 00:00:00+00:00', '2026-10-25T02:00:00+02:00'], ['2026-10-25 01:00:00+00:00', '2026-10-25T02:00:00+01:00']] (per flat). UTC has no gap or repeat, so all joins use UTC; local hour/day fields are derived from UTC.
- Consequence: local date 2026-10-25 has 25 hours, 2026-10-01 has 22 hours (data start at 02:00 local) and 2026-10-29 has 2 hours. Daily statistics use only complete local days and are reported per hour where day lengths differ.

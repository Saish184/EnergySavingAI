# Energy-saving opportunity validation

_Generated by `analysis/opportunities.py` from `data/processed/analytical_table.csv`. Machine-readable twin: [`opportunities.json`](opportunities.json). Do not edit by hand; re-run the script._

**How to read money values:** every SEK value is the **synthetic spot-energy component only** (excl. VAT, network fees, energy tax, markup and fixed fees). *Spot value, hourly tariff* applies only to students on an hourly spot-price contract. *Time-average proxy* (kWh × 0.4777 SEK/kWh) roughly represents a monthly-average spot contract. On a fixed price, multiply kWh by the student's own price; if electricity is included in rent, the student saves 0 SEK. Load shifting is worth 0 SEK on anything except an hourly tariff. None of these is a bill saving.

**Evidence vocabulary:** *measured* = read from cleaned observations; *inferred* = derived from measured patterns by an explicit rule; *hypothetical* = depends on an untestable assumption. Statistical confidence (is the pattern real?) is reported separately from action feasibility (can the student act?).

## 1. Timestamp coverage and the 672 vs 673-hour issue

- Dataset: **672 hourly intervals**, 2026-10-01T00:00:00Z to 2026-10-28T23:00:00Z (end exclusive 2026-10-29T00:00:00Z).
- Local (Europe/Stockholm): 2026-10-01T02:00:00+02:00 to 2026-10-29T00:00:00+01:00.
- The local calendar slice 1–28 Oct is **673 elapsed hours** (naive wall-clock count: 672), because 25 Oct has 25 hours. The dataset covers 671 of them, lacks 2026-10-01T00:00:00+02:00, 2026-10-01T01:00:00+02:00, and adds 2026-10-29T00:00:00+01:00.
- Keyed on naive local time the data have only **671 distinct wall-clock hours**: 2026-10-25T02:00:00+02:00 (=00:00Z) and 2026-10-25T02:00:00+01:00 (=01:00Z) collapse into one.
- Hours per local date: 1 Oct = 22, 25 Oct = 25, 29 Oct = 1, all others 24.
- The dataset is exactly 672 UTC hours. The local calendar month-slice 1-28 Oct is 673 elapsed hours because 25 Oct has 25 hours; the dataset covers 671 of them plus 00:00 on 29 Oct local, and lacks 00:00-01:59 on 1 Oct local. Anything keyed on naive local time sees only 671 distinct wall-clock hours and silently merges the two 02:00 hours. Rule: compute elapsed time, joins and totals in UTC; use local time only for labels and hour-of-day patterns; per-day comparisons must normalise by hours present (25 Oct = 25 h).

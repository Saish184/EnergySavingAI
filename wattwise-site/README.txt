Watt Wise: concept website for the AI for Energy Saving hackathon

Files
  index.html   Dashboard page (open this one first)
  mobile.html  Phone screen: tonight's tip
  styles.css   All styling, shared by both pages
  app.js       Data and interactivity (flat tabs, chart, what-if slider)

How to use
  Unzip, keep the files in the same folder, double-click index.html.
  Fonts load from Google Fonts when online; otherwise a system font is used.

Replacing the placeholder numbers
  Open app.js. The arrays BASE_PROFILE, PRICE, CARBON and FLATS at the top
  hold made-up sample data. Swap them for your real hourly averages and
  prices from the CSV analysis. Nothing else needs to change.

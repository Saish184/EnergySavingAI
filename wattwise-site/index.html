Three things to try

Shift load

Run flexible appliances after 19:00 UTC

Late-afternoon prices are among the highest of the day. Moving laundry or dishwasher cycles later keeps the same kWh but costs less. Assumes these loads are movable.

Confidence: medium
Cut waste

Check what runs overnight

A steady draw while the flat sleeps often means standby devices, a heater left on or a fridge working harder than it should. A smart plug or a quick walk-round will tell.

Confidence: medium
Possible fault

One circuit changed its pattern

If a channel jumps to a new level and stays there, it may be a faulty or stuck appliance rather than bad data. We flag it for a human to look at, never as a certain diagnosis.

Confidence: low, needs a check

How we got these numbers

Raw readings are messy, so every number passes through checks before you see it. The page shows what was fixed so you can judge how much to trust it.
